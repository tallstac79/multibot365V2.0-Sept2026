## Answers

1. **≥ 6-point discrepancies (61)**: 25 genuine lag (Bet365 updating with Pinnacle), 22 stale Bet365 markets, 8
   live/unclear, 6 single observations. None is a market-matching problem: same market, same event id, main line
   (no alt-line markers). The stale ones are comparable lines Bet365 left untouched for hours; whether they are
   still offered is only knowable at the slip — the phone's exact-line readback already refuses a moved or
   suspended line (`TARGET_NOT_FOUND` / `LINE_CHANGED`), which is the correct place for that check.
2. **Favourite flips (58 flagged, 63 accepted overall)**: 47 with Bet365 trending the same way as Pinnacle, 11
   unclear, 0 orientation errors. The fixtures the phone actually opened corroborate the sign convention:
   Besancon v Val de Seine alerts quoted Bet365 −1.5 (home) while Pinnacle had the home side +3…+4.5; the phone's
   grid on 24 Sep showed Besancon −1.5 / Val de Seine +1.5, we placed Val de Seine +1.5 (bet YT6334352221W), and by
   the morning Bet365 had followed to Besancon +2.0. Berck (Bet365 −3.5) and Orchies (LYONSO) pages agreed likewise.
   Favourite flips are legitimate line movements with Bet365 lagging; the orientation errors are the ≥ 10-point
   reversed listings, now AMBIGUOUS.
3. **REJECT = 0** because the live configuration cannot reject an implied target: `min_line_advantage` is 1.0 and
   the inference itself requires ≥ 1.0; basketball markets have no `min_price` / `max_price` / `minimum_ev`; every
   market and the global switch are enabled; `explicit_target` and `bet_quality` cannot fail once a side is implied.
   The only remaining outcomes are ACCEPT or STALE (age / event started). That is the rules engine working as the
   operator's policy layer, not an inability to reject.
4. **Proof** (`tests/test_market_audit.py`, real alerts): `min_line_advantage=5` → REJECT `line_advantage`;
   `min_price=1.9` / `max_price=1.5` → REJECT; SPREAD disabled → REJECT `market_enabled`; global disabled →
   REJECT `global_enabled`; a highlighted worse side → REJECT `bet_quality`; one year late → STALE.
5. **The 10 INVALID alerts**: all Asian Games (women) spreads on Japan v Chinese Taipei and Korea v China. Pinnacle
   shows e.g. −26.5 (alt. line), Bet365 "Spread 26.5", a Bet365 price is highlighted and `EV: 106–113 %` is supplied.
   OddsNotifier compared magnitudes and called the lines equal; Bet365 lists that pairing the other way round. Our
   signed comparison finds the lines unequal and the supplied EV contradictory → INVALID, nothing implied. Correct
   fail-closed behaviour; the phone's reversed-pairing check would refuse the event as well.
6. **Guards**: the only evidence-backed one is the reversed-listing guard already committed (opposite favourites by
   ≥ 10 points → AMBIGUOUS; 9 cases, all from fixtures whose reversed listing is proven by their own EV-supplied
   mirror alerts). Considered and **rejected**: (a) a mirror guard without EV — the 9 opposite-sign, equal-magnitude
   spreads without EV (Orchies 3.5/−3.5, Castro 2/−2, Zlin 1/−1 …) are coincidences inside genuine lag runs and would
   be lost; (b) any maximum advantage — the ≥ 6 cases are comparable lines and the slip readback is the right
   arbiter; (c) an alternate-line guard — no such alerts exist yet (0 of 427). Pinnacle movement remains diagnostic.
